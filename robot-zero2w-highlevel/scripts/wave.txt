# WAVE - sit back and wave the front-left leg
# Starts and ends in neutral stand.
#
# Why it sits first: lifting one leg leaves 3 feet. In the X stance the
# body's center sits exactly on the line between the two diagonal feet
# (front-right to rear-left), so with front-left up it tips onto that leg.
# Dropping the rear moves the weight backward, off that line, so it stays up.

# --- Stand (in case it isn't already) ---
SERVO QU 45
SERVO EU 135
SERVO ZU 135
SERVO CU 45
SERVO QL 0
SERVO EL 180
SERVO ZL 180
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 200

# --- Sit: rear feet rotate up 70, rear of body drops, weight shifts back ---
SERVO ZL 145
SERVO CL 35
DELAY 300

# --- Brace: step front-right foot forward/inward so it sits under the body more ---
# Lift front-right
SERVO EL 140
DELAY 200
# Swing it 30 forward (forward = increase for EU)
SERVO EU 165
DELAY 200
# Plant it
SERVO EL 180
DELAY 200

# --- Raise front-left above horizontal (90 = horizontal, 120 = pointing up a bit) ---
SERVO QL 120
DELAY 300

# --- Wave: swing the raised leg back and forth (QU: lower = forward) ---
SERVO QU 15
DELAY 200
SERVO QU 75
DELAY 200
SERVO QU 15
DELAY 200
SERVO QU 75
DELAY 200
SERVO QU 15
DELAY 200
SERVO QU 75
DELAY 200
# Back to center
SERVO QU 45
DELAY 200

# --- Lower front-left back down ---
SERVO QL 0
DELAY 300

# --- Undo the brace: front-right back to neutral ---
SERVO EL 140
DELAY 200
SERVO EU 135
DELAY 200
SERVO EL 180
DELAY 200

# --- Stand up: rear feet back to straight down ---
SERVO ZL 180
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 300
