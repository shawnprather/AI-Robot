# PUSHUP - all feet rotate up together, lowering the body, then back down
# Starts and ends in neutral stand.

# Lower body (all feet toward horizontal)
SERVO QL 50
SERVO EL 130
SERVO ZL 130
SERVO CL 50
DELAY 200

# Hold at the bottom
DELAY 300

# Push back up
SERVO QL 0
SERVO EL 180
SERVO ZL 180
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 200

# Hold at the top
DELAY 300
