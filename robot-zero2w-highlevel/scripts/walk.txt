# ==========================================================
# Quadruped diagonal trot - single walk cycle
# Names: Q=front-left  E=front-right  Z=rear-left  C=rear-right
#        U=hip (swings fwd/back)  L=foot (lifts/plants)
# Pair A = QU QL CU CL (front-left + rear-right)
# Pair B = EU EL ZU ZL (front-right + rear-left)
# Hip forward:  QU,ZU decrease | EU,CU increase
# Foot lift:    QL,CL increase | EL,ZL decrease
# ==========================================================

# --- STEP 1: STAND (all neutral, X-shaped crab stance, feet straight down) ---
SERVO QU 45
SERVO EU 135
SERVO ZU 135
SERVO CU 45
SERVO QL 0
SERVO EL 180
SERVO ZL 180
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 2: Lift pair A (front-left + rear-right feet up 40) ---
SERVO QL 40
SERVO CL 40
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 3: Pair A swings forward 20 in the air; pair B pushes back 20 to move body forward ---
SERVO QU 25
SERVO CU 65
SERVO EU 115
SERVO ZU 155
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 4: Plant pair A ---
SERVO QL 0
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 5: Lift pair B (front-right + rear-left feet up 40) ---
SERVO EL 140
SERVO ZL 140
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 6: Pair B swings to +20 forward in the air; pair A pushes back to -20 ---
SERVO EU 155
SERVO ZU 115
SERVO QU 65
SERVO CU 25
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 7: Plant pair B ---
SERVO EL 180
SERVO ZL 180
DELAY 200

# --- STEP 8: RETURN TO STAND (one pair at a time, so a diagonal always stays planted) ---
# Lift A
SERVO QL 40
SERVO CL 40
DELAY 200
# Center A hips
SERVO QU 45
SERVO CU 45
DELAY 200
# Plant A
SERVO QL 0
SERVO CL 0
DELAY 200
# Lift B
SERVO EL 140
SERVO ZL 140
DELAY 200
# Center B hips
SERVO EU 135
SERVO ZU 135
DELAY 200
# Plant B
SERVO EL 180
SERVO ZL 180
DELAY 200

# Done - back in neutral stand
